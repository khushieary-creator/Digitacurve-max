FAQs

Questions, answered

What is performance marketing? How can it benefit my business? +
Performance marketing is a data-driven approach to digital advertising that focuses on driving specific actions or measurable results, such as clicks, conversions or sales. It benefits your business by providing a clear return on investment (ROI) and allows you to optimise your marketing efforts based on actual performance data. It ensures that your marketing budget is spent efficiently and effectively.
How does Digitacurve approach performance marketing for my business? +
We build end-to-end acquisition funnels connecting search intent Google Ads, pre-qualifying multi-step landing page forms (collecting budget & timing), creative-led Meta campaigns, and automated CRM lead routing.
What metrics and KPIs do you track to measure the success of performance marketing campaigns? +
We focus on real business metrics rather than vanity clicks: Cost Per Qualified Lead (CPL), Return On Ad Spend (ROAS), Customer Acquisition Cost (CAC), Conversion Rate (CVR), and direct revenue attribution.
Can Digitacurve help with optimising landing pages and conversion rate optimisation (CRO)? +
Yes, we conduct thorough landing page UX audits, implement A/B split testing, optimize mobile load speeds, and redesign forms to significantly increase your conversion rate.
How much should I spend on Google Ads & Meta Ads? +
Ad budget depends on your target industry CPC and lead volume goals. We recommend an initial monthly budget of at least ₹50,000 to ₹1,00,000 to allow statistically significant keyword testing and conversion data collection.
